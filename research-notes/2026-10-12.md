### Access Paths for Efficient Ordering with Large Language Models

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-10-12
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.00303
- Matched layers: market_context

From our evaluations, we observe a general scaling relationship between sorting cost and the ordering quality for comparison-based algorithms. Building on these insights, we design a budget-aware optimizer that utilizes heuristic rules, LLM-as-Judge evaluation, and consensus aggregation to dynamically select the near-optimal access path for LLM ORDER BY.

### Adaptive DHiSparse: Speculative Decoding over a Load-Adaptive KV Hierarchy

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-10-12
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13708
- Matched layers: ai_compute

We present Adaptive DHiSparse, which supports speculative decoding over hierarchical sparse KV and adapts residency to load. On DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash at 500K context and concurrency 512, Adaptive DHiSparse with DSpark delivers 1.85$\times$ the output of resident serving with DSpark.
