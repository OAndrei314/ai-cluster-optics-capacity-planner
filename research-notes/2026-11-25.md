### Adaptive Electromagnetic Transient-level Digital Twins for Data Centers via In-Context Learning with LLMs

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-11-25
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32882
- Matched layers: data_center_energy, ai_compute

Digital twins address this computational bottleneck by emulating the behavior of a physical system and enabling fast prediction in place of expensive high-fidelity simulation. To address this limitation, this paper proposes an adaptive EMT-level digital twin for DCs based on in-context learning (ICL) with large language models (LLMs).

### A Table Is Worth 64 Tokens: Pixel-level Compression for Multi-Table Document Question Answering

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-11-25
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26949
- Matched layers: market_context

We study pixel-level table compression for question answering over documents with multiple tables, evaluating seven VLMs across two benchmarks and five visual-token budgets. Yet even at 64 visual tokens per table, tables preserve enough signal to identify whether they are relevant to a question.
