### A research world model of power engineering learns its dynamics and reveals what-ifs

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-11-04
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04566
- Matched layers: data_center_energy, ai_compute

Here we introduce research world models, which treat a research field as a dynamical system that can be acted upon, and establish one for power engineering from 1.1 million papers. By revealing the latent dynamics of a research field and its response to interventions, research world models offer a new way to simulate and test research decisions before they are made.
